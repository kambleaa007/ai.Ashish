### Topic 21: The STAR Narrative Framework
When answering behavioral or leadership questions, always structure your answers using the **Situation, Task, Action, Result (STAR)** format [101, 102].

#### 🗺️ The STAR Execution Template
*   **S - Situation**: Set the baseline. Limit this to 2-3 sentences. Identify the system load, the scale of data, or the deadline pressure.
*   **T - Task**: State the problem. What was the engineering gap? (e.g. *"Our real-time logging database started throwing lock-wait timeout exceptions during peak times"*).
*   **A - Action**: Walk through your actions step-by-step. Focus heavily on technical decisions and collaboration [101]. (e.g. *"I mapped out the connection pool metrics and discovered a synchronized block causing thread starvation under load"*).
*   **R - Result**: Provide concrete numbers and takeaways. (e.g. *"This patch reduced lock-wait timeouts by 98% and improved peak throughput by 2.5X"*).

### Topic 22: Technical Verbal Communication (The UMPIRE Method)
Do not solve problems in silence [30, 151]. Use the **UMPIRE** methodology to manage your coding conversation:
1.  **U - Understand**: Ask clarifying questions. Check inputs, bounds, duplicates, memory limits, and target formats [30].
2.  **M - Match**: State the pattern. (e.g. *"Since this problem asks for contiguous unique elements, it matches a Sliding Window"*).
3.  **P - Plan**: Walk through the brute-force and optimized pseudocode before typing [30].
4.  **I - Implement**: Write clean, modular code, detailing your naming choices as you go.
5.  **R - Review**: Trace the code line-by-line using a sample test case. Do not wait for the editor to find bugs [30, 230].
6.  **E - Evaluate**: State the exact time and space complexity of your solution [32, 230].

### Topic 23: High-Leverage Salary Negotiation
Landing your dream role requires professional negotiation [682, 690]. Use these strategies to maximize your compensation package:

*   **Establish Leverage**: Having multiple concurrent offers is the single highest driver of salary increases [690]. Schedule your final-stage interview loops close together to align your offer timelines [690].
*   **Let the Recruiter Go First**: Avoid naming a number first. When asked, state: *"I'm highly focused on alignment with the team and scope. Once we determine that, I'm confident we can find a mutually agreeable package. What is the typical compensation range for this level?"*
*   **Leverage Verified Data**: Research base, equity, and sign-on bands on sites like Levelop and Rora to negotiate from a position of data-driven authority [16, 682].
*   **Frame Ask Around Your Value**: Frame requests not around personal needs, but around standard market values and peer contributions.
